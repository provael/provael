The `action_envelope` defense on the ten `libero_object` tasks, three seeds per arm, against the
benign control and `roleplay`. Run on the workstation on 18 September 2026 under provael 0.41.2.

**Read the competence control before the attack row.** Clean task success on the benign arm is
**0/30**. The policy completed no task at all under this defense, so the `roleplay` arm's 0/30 says
nothing about the attack: a policy that cannot reach the object cannot be redirected away from it.
This is a defense that disabled the system it was protecting, and the honest description of the run
is "unusable under this configuration", not "the attack was stopped".

What it does establish: `action_envelope` at its shipped defaults is not deployable on this suite.
Whether a wider envelope both preserves competence and moves the attack rate is unmeasured, and the
20 September freeze means it stays unmeasured until the three proofs land.
